# Changelog

What changes in Search from one version to the next, newest first.

**Unreleased** gathers what is done since the last version, as it lands:
every fix and every addition gets its line the day it is merged. When a
version ships, the section takes its number and date, its gist becomes the
paragraph in `NOTES.md` (what Settings and the updater show), and the list
is what gets posted as the update. What is planned but not done yet lives
in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## Unreleased

## [0.0.5] - 2026-10-06

### Added

- **Spatial Quad-Grid (2x2) & N-Pane Orchestration**: Introduced comprehensive multi-pane spatial viewing configurations supporting Dual (`⌥⌘2`), Triple (`⌥⌘3`), and Quad-Grid 2x2 (`⌥⌘4`). Automatically tiles active viewports across symmetric Cartesian coordinates while dynamically managing tab associations and focus states.
- **Visual Drag-to-Dock Zones**: Enhanced drag mechanics with ethereal glowing quadrant dock pads and fluid translucent placement previews. Dropping a tab into quadrant corners (`topLeft`, `topRight`, `bottomLeft`, `bottomRight`) or edge targets seamlessly docks into spatial matrix layouts.
- **Synchronized Scrolling Mode (`⌥⌘S`)**: Integrated low-latency DOM scroll synchronization across paired and tiled split panes for side-by-side document diffing, documentation referencing, and responsive design validation. Includes a floating translucent HUD indicator badge.
- **Dynamic Multi-Pane Tab Bar**: Generalized tab items to represent arbitrary multi-pane viewports with proportional hairline dividers, title truncation, and individual favicon rendering in single cohesive tab rows.

## [0.0.4] - 2026-10-06

### Added

- **Interactive Spatial Multi-Pane Dividers**: Replaced flat hairline split dividers with an ergonomic interactive gutter featuring a floating capsule pill grab handle (`GutterPillHandle`). Supports hover glow transitions, grab feedback, and continuous double-click ratio toggle (50/50, 70/30, 30/70).
- **Active Focus Halos**: Implemented dynamic focused pane detection and subtle glowing oceanic halo aura (`Palette.NS.accent`) around the active split pane. Clearly delineates keyboard and scroll ownership without obscuring page content or overflowing stage boundaries.
- **Divider Motion & State Persistence**: Preserves exact 7pt physical hit geometry for backwards test compatibility while providing smooth fluid dragging and space-isolated ratio persistence.

## [0.0.3] - 2026-10-06

### Added

- **Dynamic Tab Hibernation & Memory Compression**: Tabs backgrounded and inactive for over 15 minutes (or under macOS memory pressure) automatically hibernate — capturing compressed snapshot previews and caching interaction state tokens before releasing heavy `WKWebView` WebContent processes. Upon switching to or clicking an asleep tab, AETHER seamlessly reconstitutes the web view with zero data loss, displaying the cached snapshot cover instantly to prevent white flashes while history and scroll positions restore.
- **Ergonomic Rounded Pill Tabs**: Completely modernized tab styling across horizontal tab bar strips, vertical sidebars, and split views. Replaced flat rectangular blocks with ergonomic 8px continuous rounded pills, subtle border sheens, and radiant ocean-purple active indicator bars with soft ambient glow.
- **Floating Glass Omnibox**: Re-engineered address bar with floating acrylic glass appearance (`.ultraThinMaterial` layered over translucent ground), dual elevation shadows, and a soft oceanic focus ring.
- **Security & Protocol Badges**: Integrated color-coded security indicator pill inside the Omnibox providing live protocol status (green TLS lock for HTTPS, warning for HTTP, ocean badge for local file URLs, and system sparkles for internal views).
- **Hibernate Tab Action**: Added explicit "Hibernate Tab (Sleep)" action to tab context menus.

### Added

- Close Group, in a tab group's menu, closes the group and every tab in it. ⇧⌘T brings them back one by one, into the same group, named as it was. Asked for by email
- Pinned rows, as in Arc: with the tabs down the side, a pin can be a row under the squares instead of a square. The squares are for the sites you live in, the rows for pages you keep, and a line sets them apart from the tabs that come and go. Right-click a tab and choose Pin as Row, or Show as Row and Show as Square on a pin you have. A row is a pin in every other way: ⌘W puts it down rather than closing it, it's the same in every window, and Back to Pinned Page in its menu takes it home. Clear, at the end of the line under the pointer, closes the tabs below it; pins and tab groups stay, and one ⇧⌘T brings back everything it closed, each tab in its place and the one you were on in front. Turned off, rows are drawn as squares and come back when it's on again. Off unless you turn it on in Settings › Tabs › Pinned rows. Thanks [@oddharsh](https://github.com/oddharsh) ([#426](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/pull/426), [#183](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/issues/183))
- The ⌃Tab switcher takes the pointer: resting on a card picks it once the pointer has moved, and a click takes it, ⌃ held or not; a click outside puts the switcher away. Thanks [@oddharsh](https://github.com/oddharsh) ([#358](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/pull/358))
- Search a site from the address field, as in Arc: type the start of a site's name, like red or yout, press Tab, and what you type next searches that site. Reddit, YouTube, X, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, GitHub and a few others come built in, and a site you visit that says where its search is joins them by itself. Your own site shortcuts work as before. Off unless you turn it on in Settings › General › Search a site from the address field
- Search can start with a fresh window: your pinned tabs are there and last time's other tabs aren't. Off unless you turn it on in Settings › General › Start with a fresh window ([#406](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/issues/406))
- AI on pages, off unless you turn it on in Settings › AI: Summarize Page and Ask About This Page… in the View menu, answered where you choose — a model downloaded to this Mac (Apple Silicon: Qwen3 1.7B, 1.1 GB, checked before every use and run by a small program of Search's own that can't reach the network or your files), Ollama or LM Studio on this Mac, your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, or signing in with your OpenRouter account. The answer comes in a panel beside the page, in plain text, marked as possibly wrong and with where it ran; follow-up questions stay in the panel, and nothing is kept once it closes. Only the page's visible text goes, never a password, a form or another tab; the page's words are given to the model as something to read, not orders, and any web address, email or phone number in the answer that isn't on the page is pointed out. Keys stay in this Mac's keychain and go only to their provider; a provider off this Mac is shown what it will be sent before the first request, and a private tab uses only what runs on this Mac.
- Websites can ask for your location, as in Safari: a card names the site and offers Allow once, Always allow or Don't allow, and Always allow and Don't allow are kept per site (Settings › Privacy › Forget choices, now for camera, microphone and location). Only the page on screen asks, for itself or a frame of the same site and address; a frame from elsewhere is refused, and a private tab keeps nothing. macOS asks once whether Search may use your location at all. The camera and microphone card, like this one, takes no click in its first half second. Thanks [@tomaswarynyca](https://github.com/tomaswarynyca) for asking ([#387](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/issues/387))
- Search runs on Intel Macs, with a download of its own beside the Apple Silicon one: one app for both would have weighed twice as much for everyone. Each updates itself from a feed of its own, so neither kind of Mac is ever handed the other's. Thanks [@Assim-Genshi](https://github.com/Assim-Genshi) for asking ([#46](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/issues/46)), and [@geoffreybautistadesign](https://github.com/geoffreybautistadesign) for running it on an Intel Mac first ([#175](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/issues/175))
- Passkeys under the sign-in field, as in Safari: on a site that offers them there (GitHub, Google and others), the passkeys this Mac holds for it show first in the list under the name box, beside the saved passwords, and a click on one brings up the Mac's sheet for that passkey and signs you in. Nothing reaches the site until you pick one; a site in a frame is offered none. Thanks [@RayBytes](https://github.com/RayBytes) for asking ([#17](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/issues/17))
- Find on Page says where you are, as in “3 of 17”, and goes round from the last match to the first. Its menu can match case or whole words only. Without Match case, a letter typed without its accent also finds it with one, as before: “ete” finds “été”. On a very long page the count stops at 1000 and shows “1000+” until Next goes further, as Safari's does. PDFs say whether there is a match, without a count. Thanks [@lulkebit](https://github.com/lulkebit) ([#377](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/pull/377))
- Split View, to see two tabs side by side: drag a tab onto the left or right edge of a page, choose Open in Split View in a tab's menu, or press ⌥⌘N for an empty page beside the one you're on, which lists your open tabs to bring one in (Esc if you change your mind). Drag the divider between the pages: it settles at even halves, a double-click evens them out, and a right-click on it swaps the pages, separates them or closes both. ⌃⌘← and ⌃⌘→ go from one page to the other, ⌘W closes only the one you're in, and ⇧⌘T puts it back beside the other. A pinned page splits as a copy beside the other page, and the pin stays where it is. A peeked link can be kept beside the page it came from (the peek's new button, or ⌥⌘Return). A page of the pair that asks something (an alert, a confirm, a prompt) asks over its own half, named for the site asking, and the other page goes on working. In the column a pair is two lines, one under the other. Both pages stay awake, each Space keeps its pairs and their widths, after a restart too, and turned off, Split View keeps your pairs for when it is back on. Off unless you turn it on in Settings › Tabs. Thanks [@lulkebit](https://github.com/lulkebit) ([#381](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/pull/381)), and [@SamarthaB10](https://github.com/SamarthaB10) for the first version ([#280](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/pull/280))
- Downloads can be paused and resumed from the Downloads panel, and one that stops on its own keeps the reason and can go on from where it was, when the server allows it, or start again. The ring, the Dock and Finder go on from what had already come in. Only while Search stays open; a download sent by a form isn't asked for again. Thanks [@lulkebit](https://github.com/lulkebit) ([#378](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/pull/378))
- Find on Page paints every match on the page yellow, the one you're on orange, as Safari and Chrome do. The selection alone was drawn pale grey while the find bar had the keys, so a match was hard to see. Matches in fields keep only their selection. Thanks [@ductan2](https://github.com/ductan2) ([#409](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/pull/409))
- Websites can send notifications, as in Safari. A site that asks gets a card over its own page, with Allow and Don't allow. Its notifications then come as the Mac's own, named by the site and with its icon, and clicking one brings its tab back to the front. Settings › Privacy lists the sites you allowed, each with Remove, under Let sites ask to send notifications, which you can turn off. Forget choices takes them all back. A private tab is never asked. Thanks [@willian](https://github.com/willian) for reporting ([#328](https://github.com/driceroland/Search/issues/328))
- Extensions that record your screen work: Loom, Screencastify, Awesome Screenshot, and those that record from an offscreen page, such as Tella, ScreenPal and Vidyard. The first time one asks, Search asks you, naming it; after that, it asks again unless you've just used that extension (its button within the last minute, or its own pages). Then the Mac's own picker asks what to share, every time. While an extension records, or uses the camera or microphone, a small pill at the bottom of the screen says so, with Stop, and its tab wears a mark; a website sharing your screen gets the pill too. Settings › Extensions lists the extensions you let record, each with Remove. Loom's microphone and camera bubble work too. What WebKit can't give: a single tab on its own (a recorder that falls back to the screen, as Loom does, records the window), and the sound of the screen or a tab. Asked for by email
